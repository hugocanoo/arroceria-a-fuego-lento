The house

Rice rules

A Fuego Lento is a rice restaurant in Poblats Marítims, two streets from Malvarrosa beach. Each rice dish is made one at a time, to order — that's why it takes a good half hour, and why it comes out the way it should.

The menu blends classic Valencian rice with the occasional Sicilian touch, like the titaina with caponata and northern albacore belly.

Over fire, one at a time
